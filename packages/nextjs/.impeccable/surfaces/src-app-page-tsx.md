# Surface brief — landing `/` (Agro Trazabilidad)

Scope: **replacement visual world** for the landing route (same product truth; new look).
Visitor mode: **Persuade**.
Target: `src/app/page.tsx`

**Job / audience:** external agri prospects (producers, contractors, agro companies) deciding whether this is a real, serious tool. They must understand the offer in one viewport and take an action.
**Proof to demonstrate:** offline-first field capture AND end-to-end traceability (firm → field → lot → input). No invented claims, customers, or metrics.

## Direction contract
- **THESIS:** The landing is an agronomic campaign almanac — the season is the reading order and the loop field → office → client is dated ledger entries — and it refuses the gradient-hero + card-grid that every SaaS landing ships.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Cool ruled paper white (not cream); deep ink (near-black with a green cast); a red-pencil annotation accent; a second deep field-green accent. Type: an editorial workhorse serif for headings and the ledger, small-caps labels, tabular figures; a plain sans for UI. Real field photographs mounted like prints with ruled captions. Ruled grids, ledger lines, margin annotations. The current "Agro-tech premium" world (forest-green gradient + Fraunces) is the **anti-reference**, kept only as evidence of the subject.
- **STORY:** The visitor understands this is a real tool for the field campaign that captures offline and traces from firm to lot, and moves to **see the demo** or start a **WhatsApp** conversation.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** An **almanac spread** — left column a "Campaña 2025/26" register with the loop as dated entries (campo → oficina → cliente) and the primary action set as a register line (not a floating pill); right column a real field photograph mounted as a plate with a ruled caption and a tabular data block. The offer is legible, the action is unmistakable, and offline-first + traceability are demonstrated, not just asserted.
- **FORM:** The assigned grounded direction — agronomic almanac / campaign ledger (index 4 of 7), seed key `428f04d0`. Code-led (no image generation).
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Scope note
The new world applies to the **pre-SaaS surfaces** (landing first, then demo/login/onboarding). The authenticated dashboard keeps the incumbent system and MUST NOT be broken.
